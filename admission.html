Welcome to Admissions

Nakatoooke High School welcomes students who are eager to learn, grow and achieve academic excellence. We provide an environment that encourages discipline, confidence, teamwork and success.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed admission application form.
  • Previous school academic reports.
  • Birth certificate or other identification document.
  • Passport-size photographs.
  • Transfer documents where applicable.

Our Admission Process

Step Activity
01 Visit the school or contact the administration.
02 Obtain and complete the admission form.
03 Submit the required documents.
04 Receive admission guidance from the school.

Why Choose Our School?

  • Co-curricular activities.
