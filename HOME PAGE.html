ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Welcome to Nakatooke High School.ABOUT NAKATOOKE HIGH SCHOOL Who We Are NAKATOOKE HIGH SCHOOL is an educational institution located in Bulo–Butambala, Mitala Maria, along Masaka Road, Uganda. We are committed to providing quality education, promoting discipline and nurturing responsible learners who can contribute positively to society. We believe that every student has the potential to succeed through hard work, determination, perseverance and proper guidance. Our Vision To build a strong, responsible and educated generation ready to contribute to national development. Our Mission To provide quality education in a supportive learning environment that promotes academic excellence, discipline, creativity and good character. Our Motto Perseverance We Succeed Our Core Values - Discipline - Hard work - Respect - Honesty - Responsibility - Teamwork - Perseverance - Excellence Our Academic Programmes We provide learning opportunities in both Science and Arts subjects, helping students develop knowledge, practical skills and critical thinking. Our Facilities Our school provides a supportive learning environment, including a well-built library that encourages reading, research and independent learning. Our Commitment At NAKATOOKE HIGH SCHOOL, we believe in building more than academic success. We aim to develop confident, disciplined and responsible individuals who can face future challenges and help build our country. Our Welcome Message We warmly welcome students, parents, guardians, visitors and all well-wishers to our school. Thank you for loving and supporting NAKATOOKE HIGH SCHOOL. May the Almighty bless you.
